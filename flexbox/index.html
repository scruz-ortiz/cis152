Welcome to Zoo on Camera

This is my personal gallery and reference guide for animals at the San Diego Zoo. As I visit and photograph the incredible wildlife at this world-class zoo, I document my visits and share information about the animals I encounter. Whether you're planning your own zoo visit or simply curious about animal facts, you'll find photos and details here.

About the San Diego Zoo

The San Diego Zoo is one of the world's most renowned zoological parks, home to over 3,500 animals representing more than 650 species and subspecies. Located in Balboa Park, the zoo is dedicated to animal care, conservation, and education. It provides an exceptional opportunity to observe and learn about wildlife from around the globe, all while contributing to important conservation efforts.

My Photography Journey

As a beginner photographer, I am learning and growing with each zoo visit. I primarily use my Nikon D60 digital camera to capture photos of the animals at the San Diego Zoo. I also experiment with my Minolta x370 film camera to explore the unique qualities of film photography. Both cameras present different challenges and rewards, and using them together helps me develop my skills. I focus on capturing the beauty and personality of each animal, learning about composition, lighting, and patience along the way.
