Meet Us

Pacific Trails Resort is a retreat on the North Coast designed for guests who want to spend time outdoors. Wake up to the sound of the ocean, walk a quiet trail before breakfast, and fall asleep under a canvas roof. The resort was built to share the coast's trails, water, and forest with anyone who wants to slow down and explore.

Our Story

Pacific Trails Resort began as a small hiking and nature retreat with a handful of yurts and a kitchen that served breakfast to order. Every decision since has come back to one question: will this help a guest feel more connected to the coast?

Today the resort offers five miles of hiking trails beside the state park, ocean kayaks at the water's edge, and sunrise bird watching trips several times a week. Our mission is to make the outdoors accessible to guests of all ages and to protect the natural beauty of the ocean and forest that surround us.

Our Team

Our team is small, so guests receive consistent service from check-in to the last trail map. Each member is dedicated to making your stay safe, enjoyable, and memorable.

  • Trail Guides

    Lead our guided hikes along the coast and through the forest, and know exactly where the wildflowers bloom.

  • Kayak Instructors

    Teach first-time paddlers the basics on calm water, then point experienced guests toward the best coves.

  • Bird Watching Naturalists

    Guide sunrise trips and help guests spot shorebirds, hawks, and the seasonal migrants passing through.

  • Lodge Kitchen

    Cook your breakfast to order and prepare nightly fine dining with produce from local growers.

Our Values

  • Safety first. Every trail is marked, every guide is trained, and every kayak comes with a life vest.
  • Family welcome. We plan activities for every age, so grandparents, parents, and kids can explore together.
  • Respect for nature. We leave every trail as we found it and keep our footprint small on the land we love.
  • Community. Never pass on an opportunity to make new friends, on the trail or around the lodge fire.
